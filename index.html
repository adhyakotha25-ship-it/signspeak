How SignSpeak Works

1
Hand & Body Detection

MediaPipe Holistic tracks your face, pose, and 21 hand landmarks per hand in real-time.

2
Feature Extraction

Face, pose, and hand landmarks combine so signs referencing body regions (chin, forehead, chest) work reliably.

3
Heuristic Classification

A rule-based classifier scores one whole gesture per frame using both hands plus face and upper-body pose when visible. 18 common BSL words are supported for live detection.

4
Temporal Smoothing

A rolling 12-frame vote and a short stability hold prevent accidental detections. Hold your sign steady until the bar fills.

5
100% On-Device

All processing is in your browser via WebGL. No video or data ever leaves your device.
